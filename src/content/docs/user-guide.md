<Aside type="caution" title="Alpha Software">
AgentMux is in **early alpha** and under heavy active development. Many features described here may be incomplete, unstable, or not yet implemented. Expect breaking changes between releases. We welcome feedback on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/agentmuxai/agentmux/issues) or [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/96erama9Ar).
